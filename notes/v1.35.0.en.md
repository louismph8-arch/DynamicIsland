## Opaline 1.35.0 — Crisp numbers and a smoother island

Download **Opaline-Setup.exe** below. If Opaline is already installed, it updates itself.

### 🔢 New numbers
- The island's clock, the timer, Pomodoro, ring percentages, Health and Widgets tiles, world clocks, desktop widgets and "My week" now use **Inter Display**, a free font (OFL license), in a **thin weight** for an elegant look.
- Its digits are **all the same width**: the clock and countdowns **no longer shift left and right** when a digit changes ("10:11:11" is exactly as wide as "18:48:58").

### ✨ Icons that light up
- When something is **active** (open tab, chosen settings page, checked option, Controls tile turned on, active dock cell), its icon takes **your accent color** and sits on a **small tinted pill**. You can see at a glance what's on.
- All icons come from the same family (Lucide), with the same stroke everywhere.

### 🌊 Much smoother light
- The light and reflections that follow your mouse on the island **stuttered**: they were only updated about 21 times per second. They now follow **every screen frame** (about 60 times per second).
- The **halo** around the island no longer recomputes on every light movement: it's drawn separately and kept in memory as long as the island doesn't change size.
- The look is identical; only smoothness changes. Nothing runs when the mouse is away from the island.

### 🛠️ Fixed
- **Two docks on screen**: a second dock (narrower, without background icons) could appear in front of the real one for about a minute. It was one of Opaline's **internal test** windows placing itself on screen by mistake. Fixed, with safeguards: test windows stay invisible, only one real dock can exist, and animations (Genie effect, Stage Manager swap) always close.
- **Settings window**: Windows' own buttons (minimize / maximize / close) showed on top of ours. Only ours remain, and a clean **title band** keeps content from scrolling under it.
- **AI assistant**: the Claude desktop app (Microsoft Store) is no longer mistaken for **Claude Code**, neither by automatic search nor by "Choose the file…" (a message explains which file to pick).
