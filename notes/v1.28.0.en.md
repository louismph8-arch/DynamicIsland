## Opaline 1.28.0 — Design and comfort

Download **Opaline-Setup.exe** below. If Opaline is already installed, it updates itself.

### ⚙️ Settings
- No more Windows title bar: **our own bar** (move, minimize, close).
- **Search** (Ctrl+F): type a word, Opaline takes you to the setting and makes it blink.

### 🏝️ Island
- Adjustable **size**: small, medium, large, or to the exact percent.
- **Tabs**: hide the ones you don't use, change their order, or put them **in a column**; too many tabs = they scroll.
- **Fixed / Smart / Peek modes**, like the dock: in Smart, the island hides when an app is right underneath (maximized browser…).
- **Per-app rules** for the island and the dock: e.g. Chrome = hides, Spotify = always visible.
- Fullscreen video: the island hides even if you click on another screen. **Ctrl+Alt+I** shortcut to hide / show the island.

### 🧲 Dock
- **Centered, left or right** alignment, and even smaller (down to 24 px).
- The preview close button: size and color of your choice, and a single frame around the app.
- **Hide desktop icons** (option), with a small arrow in the dock to show / hide them.

### 🌦️ Widgets and timer
- Network: “Download speed” and “Upload speed”.
- Weather refreshed when you want (every 10 min by default) and when waking from sleep; **“Detect my city”** (only if you click).
- Timer: custom duration in **hours, minutes, seconds**, end sound of your choice (Windows sounds or your own) and adjustable volume.
