## Opaline 1.37.0 — The island becomes a grid of tiles

Download **Opaline-Setup.exe** below. If Opaline is already installed, it updates itself.

### 🧩 A grid of tiles instead of tabs
- When the island opens, it now shows a **grid of tiles** arranged in **pages** (8 cells per page). A tile can be **small** (1 cell), **wide** (2 cells) or **large** (4 cells).
- **6 pages by theme**: essentials (music, controls, calendar, weather), time, the PC, people and assistants, productivity, creation.
- **Change page**: click the **dots at the bottom right**, turn the **mouse wheel**, or drag over the dots. At the end, the page bounces gently.
- **Hover a tile**: its name shows at the bottom center. Tiles arrive in a cascade when the island opens.
- **Click a tile**: it grows to fill the island and becomes the full tool (the old tab). **"← Name"** at the top left brings you back to the grid, and the tool shrinks back into its tile.
- Your **hidden tabs** stay hidden (their tile doesn't appear), and everything that used to open a tab (palette, dock, Ctrl+Alt+T, dragged files…) opens the right tool directly.
- Prefer the old look? **Settings → Island → "Tabs"** brings it back exactly as before.

### ✨ Live tiles
- **Music**: the blurred cover in the background, the title on top, a **small visualizer** that moves with the sound, and previous / play / next right on the tile.
- **Weather**: a **sky animated from your city's real weather** (sun, drifting clouds, rain, snow, storm, fog, starry night), with the temperature in thin digits.
- **Clock** (new): a dial with a **smooth seconds hand** in your accent color.
- **Monitor** (new): **CPU and memory rings** that gently breathe (red above 85 %), plus the GPU in the large size.
- **Friends**: your online friends' avatars with a **status ring** (and "playing …" in the large size).
- **Profile** (new): your profiles as dots, **switch in one click**.
- Everything that moves stops when the island is closed, on pages you're not looking at, during your games and with "Reduce motion".

### 🆕 New tiles
- **Timer**: the time left in a ring, and 5 / 10 / 25 min or Pomodoro in one click.
- **Coffee**: keeps the PC from going to sleep (1 h on the first click, or 30 min / 2 h / always). It shows in the island's pill while it's on, and can be stopped from the palette too ("coffee stop"). It stops by itself at the end of the duration and when Opaline closes.
- **Date**, **Disk space** (red below 10 %), **Counter** (click = +1, wheel, right-click to reset), **To do** (tick your tasks in the island, add them with "todo …" in the palette).
- **Mic**: mute / unmute your mic (Discord's if it's linked) in one click.
- **Wallpaper**: a thumbnail of the current wallpaper, click = next Opaline wallpaper.
- **Capture** (Windows' Snipping tool), **Color picker** (pick a color on screen, its code is copied), **Calculator** (opens the palette ready to calculate), **QR code** of what you copied, **Voice memo** (record / stop, saved in Documents\Opaline\Mémos), **Phone** (drop a file on it to send it to your phone), **Settings**.
- The clipboard and notes also have their tile, showing the latest item or note.

### 🎵 Spotify volume
- The volume of the app that's playing is now looked for on **every audio output**, not only the default one: it works with **Voicemeeter**, a headset chosen in Spotify, or a monitor with speakers.
- If music is playing but its sound isn't coming from this PC (Spotify Connect, phone), the message now says so clearly.
