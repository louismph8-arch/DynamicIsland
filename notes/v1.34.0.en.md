## Opaline 1.34.0 — Motion

Download **Opaline-Setup.exe** below. If Opaline is already installed, it updates itself.

### 🌊 An island that opens like a liquid
- When you hover the island, it **stretches in width first** (overshooting just a little, like a drop), then **in height**, and its corners relax last.
- The content then arrives **in a cascade**: each tile appears one after another, with a small spring, going from **blurry to sharp**. Closing does the reverse, faster.

### ✨ Tiles that react
- **Hover**: the tile **tilts slightly toward your mouse** (like a 3D object) and a **light reflection follows the cursor** across it.
- **Click**: the tile **presses in a little, then bounces back**. Round buttons (play, pause…) do the same.
- This applies to tiles in the Widgets, Controls, Health, Clocks and Friends tabs.

### 📄 Tabs that slide
- Switching tabs **slides the page** in the direction you go, with a bit of momentum (top to bottom if your tabs are in a column).
- With the mouse wheel, going **past the first or last tab** gives the row a **small elastic bounce**.

### 🔍 Tools open from their tile
- In the **Widgets** tab, click the **CPU, Memory or GPU** tile: it **grows to fill the island** and opens the **Health** tab. Going back to Widgets, the tool **shrinks back into its tile**. (This motion will be used by all tools in upcoming versions.)

### ♿ Accessibility
Two new settings in **Settings → Appearance → Accessibility** (or search them with Ctrl+F):
- **Reduce motion**: no more cascade, tilt, bounce, slide or reflection; everything opens with a short motion without bounce.
- **Reduce transparency**: the glass becomes almost opaque, without blurred wallpaper or grain, for more comfortable reading.

By default, they **follow Windows' settings** ("Animation effects" and "Transparency effects"), and change by themselves if you change them in Windows.

### ⚡ Still light
- Nothing runs while the island is at rest: animations compute nothing outside the moment they play.
- Everything is off during your games (Gaming mode).
- Bonus: the open island with a seasonal theme (Halloween…) now uses **3 times less** CPU.
