## Opaline 1.31.5 — Fixes

Download **Opaline-Setup.exe** below. If Opaline is already installed, it updates itself.

### 🛠️ Fixed
- The **NVIDIA** overlay (Alt+Z) is no longer mistaken for a game: the island, dock and Stage Manager no longer hide for nothing on the main screen.
- The island that follows the mouse no longer goes to a screen with a game or a full-screen video, and no longer jumps when you cross a screen.

### 🪟 Stage Manager
- The thumbnail column **hides by itself**; a small handle on the left edge brings it out (hover or click).
- The window in the middle **fills the screen** ("Apps fill the screen" option).
