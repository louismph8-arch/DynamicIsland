## Opaline 1.36.0 — A livelier island that translates

Download **Opaline-Setup.exe** below. If Opaline is already installed, it updates itself.

### 💊 A simpler compact island
- **At rest**, the pill now only shows **the time** (in thin digits), and it's narrower. The small camera / mic dots stay, discreet. Battery only appears if your PC has one.
- **During an activity**, the pill widens smoothly and shows **a progress ring and a value**:
  - timer and Pomodoro (time left);
  - sending or receiving a file with your phone (percentage);
  - Windows updates installing (download then install);
  - an AI assistant answer in progress;
  - music (cover + equalizer) and Discord voice channel (avatars).
- With **several activities** at once, the most important one shows with a small **"+1"**.
- During an activity, the **iridescent border "breathes"** gently. Never at rest, and it turns off with "Reduce motion" and during your games.

### 🌍 New: the Translate tab
- **A text**: copy it (Ctrl+C), then in the **Translate** tab click **"Translate what I copied"**. Even faster, press **Ctrl+Alt+T** right after copying. The translation is written as it comes, with **"Copy translation"**.
- The **source language is detected** automatically ("English → French"). Pick the target language (15 languages) and swap them with **⇄**.
- **In the palette** (Ctrl+Space): `tr hello everyone`, `tr to German: …`, `tr en français : …`.
- **A whole web page**: **"Translate the open page"** button. Opaline reads the address of your browser's active tab (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Vivaldi, Firefox) and opens it translated by **Google Translate**. You can also type `tr https://…` in the palette.
- Texts are translated by **your AI assistant, already set up** (nothing to add). Nothing is translated by itself: only when you ask. Passwords copied from a password manager and private pages (files on your PC, local network, sign-in pages) are **never sent**.
- Settings: **Settings → Island → Translate** (language, shortcut).

### 🎵 Music
- **Jump to any moment in the song**: the progress bar is now a **seek bar**. Click to jump, or drag while seeing the time then release. The **mouse wheel** moves 5 seconds back or forward. (If the player doesn't allow it, the bar stays plain.)
- **Spotify's sound** (or the app that's playing): the **wheel** changes the volume 2 % at a time, and clicking the icon **mutes the app**. If its sound can't be found yet, a message explains it instead of a slider that does nothing.
- The **desktop music widget** also gets a small volume control.

### 🎧 Discord voice channel
- For **each person** in the channel: a **"mute for me"** button and their **volume from 0 to 200 %** (slider or wheel). Right-click an avatar: Mute / Unmute, 50 / 100 / 150 %, "Reset to 100 %".
- At the top of the tab: **your mic** and **your headset** in one click.
- Like in Discord, these settings are **only for you**.
- If Opaline isn't allowed to change sound in Discord yet, a banner offers **"Reconnect to Discord"**.

### 🪟 Window previews in the dock
- When hovering a dock icon, the **window thumbnail now fills the whole card**, at its own shape (before, it was tiny in the middle). The extra title strip is gone: one clean card, with the title and the "×".
- Several windows of the same app show **side by side**, and the one you hover lights up.
