## Opaline 1.33.1 — A smoother Stage Manager

Download **Opaline-Setup.exe** below. If Opaline is already installed, it updates itself.

### 🪟 Window swapping, rebuilt from scratch
When you click a thumbnail in the Stage Manager column, the swap is now **smooth and natural**, like on a Mac:
- **The chosen window grows and slides** from its thumbnail to its place, with a light shadow, in a bit over a third of a second.
- **At the same time, the old window shrinks** and slips into the column, slightly faded (or toward the edge if the column hides by itself).
- **The real window only appears at the end**, already at the right size and place: no more windows that jump or correct themselves afterwards.
- **The column updates gently**: the new thumbnail fades in where the old one was, without the whole column moving.
- Windows' own minimize animation is paused during the swap (so it doesn't mix with ours), then restored automatically.

### ✨ Small extras
- If you click another thumbnail during a swap, the current one finishes right away and the next one starts cleanly.
- If Windows' "Animation effects" are off, or during a game on that screen, the swap is instant, without animation.
- Nothing takes focus during the animation, and your clicks go through.

*(From 1.33.0: light "white opal" theme, colors that follow your wallpaper, always-readable contrast.)*
