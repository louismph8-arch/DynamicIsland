## Opaline 1.26.0 — homemade AirDrop

Download **Opaline-Setup.exe** below. If Opaline is already installed, it updates itself.

### 📲 Phone ↔ PC transfer
- Turn it on in **Settings → Connected → File transfer** (off by default, only with your linked phones, on your local network).
- **From the phone to the PC**: photos and files sent from the remote page land on the **island's shelf**, with a little animation and a notice you can drag.
- **From the PC to the phone**: drag a shelf tile onto **“Phone”** (or right-click → “Send to phone”); the file shows up on the remote page (Apps tab, “From the PC”) with a **Download** button. It stays there for 2 hours.

### Also
- Automations (1.25.0): “When… then…” in Settings → Automations.
