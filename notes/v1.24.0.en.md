## Opaline 1.24.0 — the beta with friends

Download **Opaline-Setup.exe** below. If Opaline is already installed, it updates itself.

### 💬 Send feedback
- **Send feedback / Report a bug** (Opaline icon menu, or Settings → About): your message goes straight to Opaline's Discord channel.
- Add a **screenshot** (with a preview) and the **bug report** (no personal data) if you want. Nothing is sent until you click “Send”.

### 🧪 Beta channel
- Settings → Sharing → **Update channel**: “Stable” (recommended) or “Beta” to get new features before everyone else.

### ✨ What's new
- This screen! It shows up once after each update (and can be reopened from About).

### ⚡ Already in the latest versions
- **Nothing over your games**: the island and the dock disappear completely during a game.
- **Lighter**: animated wallpaper paused on hidden screens, seasonal particles frozen at rest, faster startup.
- **Safer**: updates checked against their SHA-256 fingerprint, public source code.
