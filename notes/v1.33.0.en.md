## Opaline 1.33.0 — Colors and white opal

Download **Opaline-Setup.exe** below. If Opaline is already installed, it updates itself.

### 🤍 New: the light "white opal" theme
Opaline now comes in **light milky glass**: pearly white surfaces, dark text, pastel iridescent reflections, softer shadows. The island, dock, palette, tiles, small windows and settings all go light.

**Where to choose:** **Settings → Appearance → Opaline theme**, or at first launch ("Your style" page):
- **Dark** (default): as before.
- **Light**: white opal, all the time.
- **Auto**: light during the day, dark at night, at **sunrise and sunset** in your city (the weather city). The card shows today's times.
- **Like Windows**: follows Windows' light / dark mode.

Switching themes **fades** smoothly, no flicker. This setting **never** touches Windows' theme: only Opaline changes.

### 🎨 Colors follow your wallpaper
- With **"Wallpaper colors"** (Settings → Appearance), the accent color **and now the glass tint** come from your wallpaper. Iridescent reflections use neighboring hues so everything stays in harmony.
- When you change wallpaper, colors **glide smoothly** to the new ones (about a second) instead of jumping.
- Prefer your own color? Turn the option off: the **"Fixed color"** card then shows the swatches and keeps your profile's color, whatever your wallpaper.

### 👀 Always readable
- Opaline **computes text contrast** on the glass (WCAG accessibility standard) and fixes it by itself when a very light or colorful wallpaper makes it hard to read: slightly more opaque veil, lighter or darker accent.

### 🪟 Stage Manager fixed
- **Apps fill the screen**: the window in the middle is now truly **maximized** (like Windows' "Maximize" button), even for Chrome, Discord and other apps that refused the imposed size.
- **No more disappearing windows**: menus, dialogs and small popup windows no longer take the middle spot or minimize the main window.
- **No more back-and-forth**: a window must stay in front for a short moment before taking the middle; cascading swaps and flicker are gone.
- Opaline's own invisible test windows are no longer mistaken for real windows.
