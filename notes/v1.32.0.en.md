## Opaline 1.32.0 — Opal glass

Download **Opaline-Setup.exe** below. If Opaline is already installed, it updates itself.

### 💎 A new look: opal glass
Opaline gets a new skin. The island, dock, palette, small windows, tiles and right-click menus are now **milky glass**, like an opal stone:
- **The glass takes your accent color** (from your profile or your wallpaper) with a **very fine grain**, for a soft look instead of a flat color.
- **Your wallpaper shows through, blurred**, behind the island and the floating dock. When a window is right behind the island, the glass gets darker so text stays easy to read.
- **Borders are iridescent**: a gradient of colors close to your accent that **slides as you move the mouse**, like an opal turning in the light.
- **A thin line of light** on top of every surface, and a soft shadow underneath.
- **A pearly sweep crosses the island** when it opens and when a notice arrives.
- **Tiles light up**: hover one and it gets a halo of its color.

### 🎚️ Three intensities to choose from
- **Subtle**: smoky glass, barely visible reflections.
- **Balanced** (default): milky glass, soft iridescent reflections.
- **Intense**: vivid opal, strong reflections and glows.

**Where to choose:** at first launch in the setup assistant ("Your style" page), or anytime in **Settings → Appearance → Opal glass**. The change is instant. The **"Discover the new style (demo)"** button shows all three side by side on your own wallpaper.

### ⚡ Still light
- Nothing animates while the island is closed and the mouse doesn't move.
- Reflections and glows turn off during your games (Gaming mode).
- If you turn off "Liquid glass" in Settings → Appearance, the animated effects turn off too.

### ℹ️ Good to know
- The blur shows your **wallpaper**, not the windows behind the island: Windows can't blur a window behind a rounded shape.
- The dock bar keeps Windows' real blur (acrylic), now tinted with opal.
