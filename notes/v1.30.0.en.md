## Opaline 1.30.0 — Focus and notes

Download **Opaline-Setup.exe** below. If Opaline is already installed, it updates itself.

### 🌙 Focus
- **Do not disturb** in the Controls tab: 30 min, 1 h, until tomorrow or until you turn it off. A small moon shows it in the island.
- **Night light**: warmer light in the evening, adjustable, at sunset or at your own hours. Your original colors always come back (exit, crash, uninstall).
- **My week** page: screen time, game time, Pomodoros, your most used apps and games, in small charts. Everything stays on your PC, and you turn it on yourself.

### 📝 Notes
- New **Notes** tab: write in our own small editor (saved automatically), checklists you can tick right in the island, colors, pins.
- From the palette: "note buy bread".

### 📋 Clipboard
- **+ New**: write a text, it's added to the history and copied. You can also edit a text you copied before.

### 🧲 Dock
- **Follow the mouse's screen** option, like the island.
